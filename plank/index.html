Now the person holds the same plank position on a straight inclined board, feet elevated toward the upper end. Both contact points sit directly on the board, so the normal forces are perpendicular to the board and friction acts along the board. It is easiest to use axes aligned with the board: x′ along the board (up-slope positive) and y′ perpendicular to the board. Resolve the weight into components along these axes: W∥ = W·sin(theta) (down-slope) and W⊥ = W·cos(theta) (into the board).
