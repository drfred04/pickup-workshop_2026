Holding Back the Water: A Gate Equilibrium Lab

A rectangular vertical gate of height h and width b (into the page) closes an opening at the bottom of a water channel wall. It is pinned at its top edge A and rests against a smooth stop at its bottom edge B. Water presses on one face. You will draw the pressure loading and the free-body diagram, replace the distributed water load with a single resultant force, write the equilibrium equations, and solve for the support reactions — then explore how those reactions change as the water gets deeper.

3. Explain the effect of the loading

Not graded, but your instructor will read these.
